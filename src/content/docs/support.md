MUSE is a writing app for novelists, screenwriters, and long-form writers.

**Email: support@lunareos.com**

We aim to reply within two business days. MUSE is currently in alpha, so please mention
which version and which device you are using — it is usually the fastest route to an answer.

## Before you write in

**Something went wrong while writing.** MUSE keeps snapshots and automatic backups of every
project. Open the project and check **Version History** before doing anything else — the
earlier text is very likely still there.

**The app closed unexpectedly.** MUSE saves a recovery draft every few seconds while a
document has unsaved changes. When you reopen the project, it offers to restore it.

**Where are my projects?** On desktop, `.muse` files stay wherever you saved them. On iPhone
and iPad, projects you created inside MUSE appear on the welcome screen; projects you opened
from iCloud Drive or Dropbox stay where they are, and you reopen them from the same place.

**I need to move a project to another device.** A `.muse` project is a single file. Copy it
like any other document — there is no export step and nothing is left behind.

## Alpha testers, please note

MUSE is not finished software, and alpha builds do not guarantee that projects carry across
updates.

**Keep a copy of anything you care about before installing a new build.** On desktop, copy
the `.muse` file somewhere safe. On iPhone and iPad, use **Share** to save it outside the
app — to Files, iCloud Drive, or your own backup. It takes seconds, and it is the difference
between an inconvenience and a lost draft.

## Reporting a problem

The more specific the report, the faster the fix. Useful details:

- What you were doing when it happened
- What you expected, and what happened instead
- Whether it happens every time or only sometimes
- Your device, operating system, and MUSE version

Screenshots and short screen recordings help enormously.

:::danger[Data-loss reports come first]
If you lose work or suspect you might have, say so plainly at the top of your message, and
**do not overwrite the backup folders next to your `.muse` project.** These go to the front
of the queue.
:::

## Privacy

The offline MUSE application stores your work on your device and does not collect usage
telemetry. Optional website registration is separate. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).

Privacy questions: **privacy@lunareos.com**

## Tester registration and email

[Tester registration](/testers/) is not open yet. When available, it will express interest
in upcoming tests; it will not guarantee acceptance or create an account required to use MUSE.

- **Stop emails:** once registration emails are enabled, use their unsubscribe link.
  Topic preferences will use the email’s preference link once configured. If you cannot
  access a link, contact **support@lunareos.com** for help.
- **Leave the testing program:** email **support@lunareos.com** and explicitly request
  departure. Leaving testing and unsubscribing from emails are separate requests.
- **Request deletion of registration information:** email **privacy@lunareos.com**.
  Deletion and any limited suppression retention are covered by the registration privacy
  notice, which must be finalized before registration opens.

Departure and deletion requests are handled by email, without a self-service account portal.
Send the request from the relevant address where possible. Do not include manuscripts,
passwords, or confirmation links. An email-address change will require confirmation of the
new address; support can help with the process once registration is available.
